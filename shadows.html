The Story

A Case Off the Record

Pythos is a planet of ten races, permanent smog, and citizens lost in the metaverse. Bill’s mornings are cigars, his secretary Anna’s strange dolls, and cases nobody else wants — until the Commissioner arrives in person with a warning: what we say here is off the record.

Bill doesn’t work alone. Justin, the symbiont who shares his mind, was a detective in his own world — made wise by eons among the stars, feeding Bill memories of blue-blooded murders on planets he’s never seen. Together they follow a vanished executive’s trail from a heavy-gravity factory moon to a station of millions of spinning tons, where a journalist knows too much and a man named Jason Ellison may hold the last thread.

And over it all hangs the rumor no one wants to believe: that an uncharted arm of the galaxy has been occupied by beings from another universe — and that the next face you recognize might be a doppelganger. When the trap closes, the only way out is escape to Mandra, and a horizon Bill never planned on.

From the Pages

The Streets of Pythos

For some reason, the city was unusually gloomy. The pollution was at its highest — you could feel it in the air that you breathed. The F.T.L. drives had spawned suddenly, causing a migration never seen in the galaxy before. Pythos, the planet we are on, had over ten races living on it.

My symbiont, during my sleep, chooses to show me images of the worlds it has visited. In his world he was somewhat a detective too. It’s weird to have all these memories and not know how they can assist me.

As soon as we dropped out of F.T.L., the station appeared in all its might — millions of tons spinning to create artificial gravity, a gateway to the galaxy. You will find merchants, people trying to cut a deal for a better life, and people like me: trying to solve a case and find the elusive truth. I know that truth is a matter of perspective.
