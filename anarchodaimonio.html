The Story

A Door at Marathon

Greece leaves its past to its destiny — cutbacks, bureaucracy, an Academy that grows tired at the thought of anything new. But the myth of Isida's pyramid at Marathon has intrigued the archaeologist Aristides too long, and with his colleague Andromache beside him, the vote against them becomes a beginning rather than an end.

What they uncover beneath the soil of Attica is an empty sarcophagus — and a door onto another era. A strange presence had already come to Aristides in his sleep the night before the decision. Now he learns what it wanted. Beyond the door wait a king's death and a People's Assembly, the aura of the desert and the waters of Hades, the meeting of Nicaea and the kiss of death itself.

To come home, Aristides must resist the call of the siren, heed the Founder's warning, and carry back a knowledge that will not leave his family untouched. Το Αναρχοδαιμόνιο is a journey through time told as a question the ancients already asked: know thyself — for the unexamined life is not worth living.

From the Pages

Without the Past There Is No Future

Archaeological sites in Greece could be better cared for. It is said that cutbacks should be made; that emphasis should be given to the country's present day, and the past left to its destiny. Without the past there is no future. Whoever doesn't possess the knowledge of his heritage is doomed to have limited awareness of oneself.

This land has offered everything from the very beginning of our history — arts, poetry and philosophy. Indeed, if someone stops to analyse what has been created, they will probably never finish. You will be lost in this mythical history. Sometimes you discover a paradox: lately we have spoken a lot about the pyramids that stand within the Greek domain, and yet, for some reason, there has been no in-depth study, no academic research. It is very strange, if one considers the severity of the subject.

The night before the decision was made, a strange presence came to me in my sleep.
