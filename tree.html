The Story

A Boy, a Telescope, a Universe

The great war left the world in the hands of a marshal, and the people followed him into the cave of ignorance where the one-eyed man reigns. Schools teach that only conservative politics can save man from his destructive nature. But in one of those schools there is a child who spends his evenings at an attic window, falling asleep on the ledge under a blanket printed with the planets.

When Harry deciphers a string of mysterious coordinates, his stargazing stops being a dream and becomes a map. What waits at the end of it is a planet that holds a single living thing — a golden tree, the tree of life on another heavenly body — and the discovery that the Muses are everywhere, like the tree itself.

His journey gathers companions with names written in the sky — Orion, whose work is heard across many worlds; Demeter, who shares his voyages to the stars; the mysterious Echetlaeus. It carries him through the veil of starlight to the frontier station of Methuselah — and home again, to two missing years he cannot remember, and a sister holding a book whose author shares her name.

From the Pages

The Attic Window

Harry at school is one of those kids who spends a lot of time alone. He divides his time between his lessons and the small telescope his father had bought him — probably the best present they had ever given him. Looking up at the stars, he felt like he belonged to another world, far from Earth; a feeling inexplicably familiar.

In the evenings, when the sky was clearer, he spent many hours looking at the constellations — Orion, Sagittarius. The small window in the attic was the bridge to the infinite universe, where he gathered his thoughts and dreams which found a foothold in another cosmic dimension. Often he would fall asleep on the window ledge while gazing at the stars.

He saw a tree with green leaves. Not only did he discover a new planet — he discovered life on it. A tree, nothing else. But it was the tree of life on another heavenly body. Life is pervasive in the universe. What mysteries did it hide?
