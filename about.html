The Author

Derek Robotis

Derek Robotis writes at the crossroads where Greek myth meets deep space. His stories begin in familiar places — a school attic, an archaeologist’s office in Athens, a cramped detective agency — and end among ancient civilizations, talking stars, and doors through time.

His four books share one universe. A boy named Harry follows coordinates to the tree of life. A visionary named Fabian digs up something older than mankind beneath the sands of Mars. A detective named Bill shares his mind with a being made wise by eons among the stars. And an archaeologist named Aristides opens an empty sarcophagus at Marathon and steps into another era.

One conviction runs through every page: without the past there is no future — and the future is written in the stars.
