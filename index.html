About

"Writing is the act of listening carefully."

Derek is a writer working at the intersection of memory, language, and identity. His work has appeared in literary journals across Europe, and he writes equally in English and Greek.

His essays explore what it means to inhabit two languages, and his fiction finds the extraordinary within the ordinary rhythms of Mediterranean life.
